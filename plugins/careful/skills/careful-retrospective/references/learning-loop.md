# Learning loop

Run a full retrospective automatically after Deep work. For Standard work, trigger it after blocks, overrides, material review/test failures, repeated rework, or direct durable feedback. Do not run it for clean Quick work unless asked.

## Candidate quality

Create a candidate only when an observed event supports it. Keep separate:

- **Observation**: what happened.
- **Evidence**: tests, review findings, diffs, user corrections, or concrete history.
- **Hypothesis**: why it happened.

```text
Candidate: <smallest proposed improvement>
Observation: <what happened>
Evidence: <support>
Hypothesis: <why this change may help>
Suggested scope: project | shared harness | mechanical check | record only
Trade-off: <new friction or cost>
Confidence: low | medium | high
Validation: <how to keep, revise, or remove after use>
```

Ask only questions needed to determine cause or scope. Never promote a candidate automatically. Keep weak or one-off lessons as hypotheses rather than rules.
